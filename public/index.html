🔍 How to find Divergence (BTC vs USDT.D)

Regular Divergence (Trend Reversal): If BTC makes a Lower Low but USDT.D fails to make a Higher High (makes a Lower High instead) ➔ Market is about to reverse (Bullish for BTC).

Hidden Divergence (Trend Continuation): If BTC is in an uptrend and pulls back to make a Higher Low, but USDT.D pushes up hard and makes a Higher High ➔ Hidden Bullish for BTC (USDT.D is exhausted, BTC will continue pumping).
